Agri Hydroponics (Hengshui Fanye Technology Co., Ltd.) was founded with a single mission: to provide professional, sustainable, and high-yield agricultural solutions to growers worldwide. Based in Hebei, China, we have grown from a local specialist into a global export powerhouse.

We specialize in the R&D, design, and precision manufacturing of complete greenhouse structures and advanced hydroponic systems. From our signature Vertical Towers to large-scale NFT installations, we provide the engineering backbone for successful commercial farms.
