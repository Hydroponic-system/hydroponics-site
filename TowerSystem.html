Smart Vertical Hydroponic Growing Tower System

FAQ

Q: Does the system include lighting?

A: Standard systems are for greenhouses with natural light. For indoor farming, we provide customized high-PAR LED lighting solutions tailored to your tower height.

Q: What is the maintenance cycle?

A: The system is mostly automated. Weekly checks on nutrient pH/EC levels and reservoir cleaning every 1-2 months are recommended.
